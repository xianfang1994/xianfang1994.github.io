Biography

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University, Hangzhou, China. I am currently also a visiting scholar at the National University of Singapore. My research interest is computer vision, specifically focusing on salient object detection, camouflaged object detection, medical image segmentation, and remote sensing image processing.
