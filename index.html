Biography

I am currently an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Sci-Tech University, Hangzhou, China. My research interest is computer vision, specifically focusing on salient object detection, camouflaged object detection, medical image segmentation, and remote sensing image processing.
